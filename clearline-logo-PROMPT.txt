Image-gen prompt for the Clearline extension icon.
Render at 1024x1024, then downscale to 128/96/48/32/16 for src/icons/.

PROMPT
------
A flat vector app icon on a solid rounded-square background, no gradient mesh,
no photorealism. The mark is a short vertical stack of three horizontal bars of
unequal length, like lines of chat text. The top bar is solid and fully opaque.
The middle bar is half faded. The bottom bar has dissolved into four or five
small squares drifting rightward and downward, as if being swept away. Generous
negative space around the mark. Two colours only plus the background. Crisp
geometric edges that survive being scaled to 16 pixels: no thin strokes below
about 6% of the canvas width, no text, no letterforms, no drop shadows, no
Discord logo or any element resembling it.

Background: deep indigo. Mark: near-white, with the dissolving squares stepping
down in opacity.

CONSTRAINTS
-----------
- Must not resemble the Discord wordmark, the Discord face, or Discord's blurple
  closely enough to read as official. Store review and trademark both care.
- Must stay legible as a 16px favicon, which is what kills most detailed marks.
- No text in the image.

WHY THIS MARK
-------------
The product is bulk clearing of your own messages. Bars dissolving left to right
reads as "messages going away under control" rather than "delete", which is the
tone the whole extension aims for: deliberate, paced, reversible in intent.
