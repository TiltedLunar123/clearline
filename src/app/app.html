    What should I do with them?

    Overwriting first means the last version anyone logged is the replacement rather than what you wrote. It doubles the number of requests, so it takes about twice as long.

    The copy is handed to your browser before the first deletion. Check it arrived before you start: Clearline does not take the downloads permission, so it cannot see where your browser put it.
